The reality

Your branches run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and hope.

Standard tools hand you a blank form and walk away. They never check the work actually got done. So the gaps only surface when an inspector is standing in your kitchen.

Checklists get forgotten

Managers are serving customers. Opening and closing lists get backfilled on Friday, defeating the entire purpose.

Incomplete safety logs

Fridge temperatures and hygiene records miss crucial data, creating real liability the moment an auditor asks.

Expired certificates

Vendor docs, pest control and staff training lapse without HQ knowing, until someone requests them.

Late incident reporting

Complaints and staff incidents sit in a manager's notebook for days before they're escalated properly.

No real-time visibility

HQ has to visit, or chase people on WhatsApp, to know whether a branch is compliant today.

Inspection-day scramble

When the officer walks in, it's a frantic hunt for the right binders and the paperwork that proves it.
